The Difference

The consultant leaves you a roadmap.
We leave you a running system.

Most corporate AI work dies in the gap between the pilot and the business. The pilot succeeds, because pilots are designed to. Then it meets real data, real staff, real procurement and real edge cases, and it quietly stops being mentioned. Nobody is at fault, because nobody was ever in the building.

Forward deployment closes that gap by refusing to open it. We work in your environment, on your data, against the constraint that is actually costing you money, from the first week. What we build is narrow on purpose, because narrow things survive contact with an organisation and grand ones do not.

We do not sell software, platforms, licences or a vendor relationship. Whatever we build runs on the models you already pay for, and you own it outright when we go. Our incentive is to make you independent, because the only thing we have to sell is the next engagement you choose to give us.

How It Runs

Four stages, and the first one is not a workshop

We keep engagements short and visible. If we cannot show you something working inside a week, the problem is either the wrong one or not ready, and we would rather find that out on day five than in month four.

Week 0

Two days inside the business

Not a discovery workshop. We sit with the people whose time is being consumed and watch the work happen. We arrive already knowing the failure patterns, because we have watched 700 executives hit the same ones.

Week 1

The first working artifact

Something real in your hands by day five. It will be narrower than you hoped and more useful than you expected. It exists to prove the shape of the problem, and to kill the idea early if the shape is wrong.

Weeks 2 to 6

Build, and hand over while building

Your people work alongside ours from the start. Every stage is a numbered file, every decision is recorded, and the whole thing leaves a governance trail your auditors and your successors can follow.

After

We leave, it keeps running

Your team owns and operates it. We stay available for the next problem rather than embedded on a permanent retainer. An engagement that cannot end was never engineering, it was dependency.

One honest caveat, because it comes up in every scoping conversation. Sometimes the right answer is that AI should not be doing this job at all, or not yet, or not on this data. We will say so, and we will say why. That answer is worth considerably more to you than another engagement is to us.
