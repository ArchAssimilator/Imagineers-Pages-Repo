Who
Imagineers.ai Is

Imagineers.ai is a South African AI executive training provider, founded in August 2023 by Rutger-Jan van Spaandonk and Chris Barker. It teaches senior leadership teams to get business-grade work out of frontier AI, and takes fractional Chief AI Officer mandates and forward-deployed engineering work for the same clients. It has no platform, no licence and no vendor margin, which is why its advice on which AI tools are worth paying for is blunt.

Who It Is For

Two kinds of buyer, and nobody else

We are deliberately narrow. If you are not one of these two, we will say so rather than take the work.

  1. Senior executives and exco members at large South African businesses. People who have to decide what AI means for their function, are getting school-grade output from tools nobody taught them to drive, and cannot afford to be the last person in the room who does not know.
  2. Boards and leadership teams that need Chief AI Officer capability without a full-time hire. They want board-level credibility and real operating experience on a standing mandate, not a deck and an invoice.
Difference

Three things that are not true of the alternatives

  1. The two founders facilitate every course themselves. There is no training faculty, no associate trainers and no franchise. If you book a cohort, you get the two people on this page, in the room, for both days.
  2. Operators before trainers. Managing Director of Apple South Africa. General Manager of Unilever's Lipton Teas. Co-founders of Neotel and Seacom SA. Leadership of South Africa's Covid critical care, testing, oxygen and vaccination programmes. The examples in the room are ones we ran, not ones we read about.
  3. Two days doing your own work, not watching slides about AI. Delegates leave having built things, including a full financial model in Excel from a plain-English brief. The test of the course is what you can do on the Monday, not what you can recall.

No platform, no licence, no vendor margin. It is the reason our answer to "should we buy this AI tool" is usually no, and why that answer is worth something.
