Executive AI Masterclass Details

Move from casual AI user to business-grade AI operator.

A two-day practical course for leaders, founders, senior managers, consultants, and business builders who want to use AI for real business work.

Course Positioning

This is not an AI theory course and it is not generic software training.

It is a business masterclass for people who need to understand what AI can do, how to direct it properly, and how to use it inside everyday work.

At a Glance

Two days with a deliberate progression.

Participants understand the technology, prompt it properly, chain work into repeatable workflows, apply it to business outputs, and see how those workflows can become reusable tools, dashboards, apps, and agents.

Day Focus Outcome
Day One Foundations, prompting, workflow design, and context management Participants move from casual AI use to structured, business-grade AI work.
Day Two Research, data, Excel, assistants, media, dashboards, apps, and agents Participants see how AI can reduce drudgery across real business processes.

Why this course matters now

Most people still underestimate AI because they have only used it at a shallow level. They ask casual questions, get generic answers, and conclude that the technology is useful but not transformative.

The course helps participants:

  • Treat AI as something to manage and direct, not merely something to query.
  • Use business judgement to brief, review, and improve AI output.
  • Understand the difference between weak AI use and high-leverage AI use.
  • Build practical skill while experimentation is still accessible.
  • Recognise where AI can remove slow, repetitive, low-leverage work.

Who should attend

This course is built for business people who need practical AI capability, not technical theory.

Executives Founders Business owners Management consultants Senior managers Strategy teams Finance teams Operations leaders HR and recruitment leaders Marketing and sales leaders Corporate innovation teams

Participants do not need to be developers or data scientists. They do need to be willing to think clearly, brief carefully, review outputs critically, and apply AI to real work.

What participants leave with
  • A practical understanding of modern AI capability.
  • A structured prompting method.
  • A repeatable way to build prompts in Markdown.
  • A way to store and reuse prompts in Obsidian.
  • A framework for chaining business workflows.
  • A better understanding of context management and context rot.
  • Working examples across research, reports, presentations, Excel, data analysis, dashboards, apps, media, custom assistants, and automation.
  • A clearer view of how AI capability can become team workflow, company knowledge, and organisational IP.
