What Is a
Fractional CAiO?

Imagineers.ai is a South African firm that provides fractional CAiO services. A fractional CAiO is a Chief AI Officer who works on a retained, part-time or standing-mandate basis for one or more organisations, rather than as a full-time employee. The role carries the same accountability as a permanent CAiO, board direction, use-case triage, vendor decisions, risk position, without the organisation carrying a full-time package.

FAQ

Questions people ask about fractional CAiOs

What does a fractional CAiO do?

A fractional CAiO sets the AI agenda, triages use cases before spend, makes platform and licence decisions, owns the data and security policy position, builds leadership and workforce AI capability, and holds a standing seat at board and executive committee level, all on a retained rather than full-time basis.

How is a fractional CAiO different from a consultant?

A consultant typically delivers a report or a project and leaves. A fractional CAiO holds an ongoing seat, accountable for the AI agenda over time, the same way a fractional CFO or fractional CTO holds a standing executive role rather than a single engagement.

How much does a fractional CAiO cost?

Cost depends on scope, cadence and the organisation involved, so fractional CAiO mandates are typically quoted individually rather than fixed. Imagineers.ai quotes its fractional CAiO mandates on enquiry.

Is a fractional CAiO right for my company?

A fractional CAiO tends to fit organisations that need board-credible AI direction but do not have, or cannot yet justify, a full-time Chief AI Officer, and that would rather buy standing judgement than a one-off report.
