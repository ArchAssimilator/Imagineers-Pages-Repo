The Fees

Two prices, both published

There is no rate card behind an enquiry form. An open enrolment seat costs R21,500 incl VAT per person, twelve seats to a room. A private team cohort costs R215,000 ex VAT for up to fifteen delegates, wherever your leadership team sits. Delivery outside South Africa is quoted on enquiry, because the travel is the variable, not the teaching.

Both fees cover both full days, both facilitators in the room, the materials, and the First Wednesday alumni session every month afterwards for as long as you want to keep coming. Travel, accommodation and parking are not included. See the two participation models for what each one includes in full.

Payment

Paid in full, before the course runs

Open enrolment seats are paid at the point of booking, through the Daily Maverick shop, because Daily Maverick holds the booking for every public cohort. Private cohorts are invoiced by Imagineers.ai and settled before the first morning.

The VAT number appears on the invoice. It is not published here.

If Plans Change

Send a colleague, or take a later cohort

Send a substitute. If the delegate who booked cannot make it, another person from the same organisation can take the seat at no charge. Tell us who is coming instead and that is the end of it. This is the outcome we prefer, because an empty chair in a room of twelve costs everybody something.

Otherwise, rebook. If nobody can attend, the seat moves to a later cohort rather than being refunded. Cohorts run regularly in Johannesburg, Cape Town and Durban, so the wait is usually weeks rather than months.

Open enrolment bookings are transacted by Daily Maverick, so their published ticket terms are the ones that govern the payment. Read them on the booking page before you buy.

Procurement

Purchase orders and vendor portals: usually no

Imagineers.ai is two people. Supplier onboarding portals, vendor registration packs and purchase order workflows cost more to service than a single cohort is worth, so the default answer is payment up front against an invoice. Ask before you book if your finance function cannot work any other way, and it will be considered, but treat a yes as the exception rather than the process.

We would rather tell you this before you spend three weeks in your own procurement system than after.

Accreditation

Not accredited, not levy-claimable, Level 4

The masterclass is not accredited by any SETA or other body, and there is no certificate at the end. That is a deliberate choice rather than an oversight: accreditation would fix the curriculum in place, and this material gets rebuilt after every course because the field moves faster than any syllabus approval cycle.

The fee therefore cannot be claimed against the skills development levy. If levy recovery is the deciding factor in your training budget, book something accredited instead. We would rather say that here than in the third meeting.

Imagineers.ai is an exempt micro enterprise, which carries an automatic Level 4 B-BBEE contributor status. A sworn affidavit confirming it is available on request for your procurement file.

Fit

Who should not book this

The two days are spent doing your own work on your own laptop, at pace, with two facilitators moving between twelve people. That format rules some people out, and it is cheaper for everybody if we say so now.

  • Do not book if you do not work at a computer for most of the working day. Everything the course teaches is a way of getting more out of the hours you already spend at a screen. If those hours are few, there is very little for the method to attach to.
  • Do not book if your computer use does not go much beyond email. Delegates who are still working out where a file saved to spend both days on the mechanics instead of the method, and they leave knowing it. Get comfortable driving a computer first, then come.
  • Do not book if you want a certificate. There is not one. See above.
  • Do not book if you need the fee to come back off the skills levy. It will not.

If you are unsure which side of that line you sit on, email masterclass@imagineers.ai and describe your working week. We will tell you plainly, including when the answer is that this is not for you.
