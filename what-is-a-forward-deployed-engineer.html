What Is a
Forward Deployed Engineer?

Imagineers.ai is a South African firm that provides forward-deployed engineering (FDE). A forward deployed engineer is an engineer who works embedded inside a client's business, in their environment and on their own data, shipping working AI systems against a real problem rather than delivering a report or a roadmap from the outside.

The Role

What a forward deployed engineer actually does

The defining feature of the role is where the work happens: inside the client's business, not on the vendor's side of the table.

Embedded

Works inside the client's environment

On the client's own systems and data, next to the people whose work is being changed, not from a separate delivery team.

Shipping

Builds against the real problem

Something working, not a slide deck. The first artifact is judged on whether it runs, not whether it presents well.

Iterating

Ships early and narrow, then widens

Small, obviously useful wins first, because narrow things survive contact with an organisation and grand ones do not.

Handover

Leaves the client owning and operating it

The engagement ends by design. What was built keeps running without the engineer still being in the room.

Imagineers.ai runs engagements of exactly this shape in South Africa, and internationally on request. See how forward deployed engineers work.

FAQ

Questions people ask about forward deployed engineers

What does a forward deployed engineer do?

A forward deployed engineer works inside a client's business, in their environment and on their own data, shipping working AI systems against the real problem from week one, rather than handing over a roadmap and leaving.

What is FDE in AI delivery?

FDE stands for forward-deployed engineering: engineers embedded inside a client's business rather than delivering a report from the outside. Imagineers.ai runs FDE engagements in South Africa, and internationally on request.

How is a forward deployed engineer different from a regular software engineer?

A regular software engineer usually builds against a specification handed to them. A forward deployed engineer works inside the client's environment, on the client's own data and constraints, and is expected to help find the right problem to build, not only build what was asked for.

How is a forward deployed engineer different from a consultant?

A consultant typically leaves a roadmap or a recommendation. A forward deployed engineer leaves a running system, built and shipped inside the client's own environment.
